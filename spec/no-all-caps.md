# no-all-caps

**Rule:** No `ALL CAPS` in UI copy, no `text-transform: uppercase`, no `tracking-widest` used to fake caps.

**Why:** Warm, editorial interfaces don't shout. Clovy's `spec/no-all-caps.md` and your SA firms agree — a compliance pack in ALL CAPS looks like a 1997 SARS fax.

**Exceptions:** None for UI. Acronyms like SARS are not "all caps" — they're names.
